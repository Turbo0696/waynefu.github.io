What "Michigan Ranks #5" Actually Tells You

Prompted by a Detroit News media request, a closer look at why Michigan's No. 5 ranking in five-year business survival is close to meaningless on its own — a rank is not a measurement, and a state's own trend against the national average tells you far more.
